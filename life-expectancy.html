Life Expectancy Analysis(G20 Countries)

Data Source:Woorld Bank,World Development Indicators

About the G20: The G20(Group of Twenty)is an international economic forum established in 1999 to bring together major advanced and emerging economics to discuss and coordinate global financial stability and policy.

Goals: To analyse life expectancy across the G20 member countries,and find out which countries rank highest and lowest,how life expectancy has changed over time,and how women and men compare.

Descriptions:This dashboard looks at life expectancy at birth for 19 G20 member countries,from 2000 to 2021.The original dataset mixed countries with regions,so i limited the analysis to the G20 countries.The European Union and African Union were left out because they are not countries.I built with PivotTables,slicers and four charts:a country ranking,a yearly trend,a female vs male comparison and a gender gap by country.All figures are simple averages,not weighted by population.

Tools: Excel,PivotTable,Slicers,Power Query.

Results:

  • Japan had the highest average life expectancy among the 19 countries(about 83 years),and South Africa the lowest(about 58).
  • Across the G20,life expectancy rose from about 73 years in 2000 to about 78 around 2019,then fell to about 76 by 2021.
  • Women live about 5 years longer than men on average(78 vs 73)
  • Russia had the widest gender gap,about 12 yearshh
